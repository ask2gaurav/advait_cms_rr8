### Page Goals
1. Clearly explain the services we offer in the AI era.
2. Educate clients on the shift from traditional hourly pricing to modern AI-aware pricing.
3. Present a clean, visual **Old vs New Pricing Strategy comparison**.
4. Make it easy for visitors to choose the right engagement model and start a conversation.
5. Structure the page so a CMS can later inject or edit content (use clear section markers / data attributes).

### Required Page Structure (in this order)

1. **Hero Section**
   - Headline: something like “Services Built for the AI Era”
   - Subheadline that mentions 20+ years of experience + AI-accelerated delivery + production RAG/Agent systems
   - Two CTAs: “Discuss Your Project” and “View Pricing Models”

2. **Services Overview** (4 cards or sections)
   - AI-Accelerated Custom Software Development
   - Production RAG, Multi-Agent & Knowledge Systems
   - End-to-End Web, IoT & Platform Engineering
   - Ongoing Management, Optimization & AI Monitoring
   Each card: short description, key outcomes, “Learn more” link

3. **Pricing Philosophy Section** (important)
   - Short explanation: “Why pricing is changing in the age of AI coding agents and LLMs”
   - Emphasize that we still deliver senior human oversight, architecture, security, and quality gates — AI accelerates the work, it does not replace accountability.

4. **Old vs New Pricing Strategy Comparison** (core section — make this visual and clear)
   Create a clean, responsive comparison that works well on mobile.

   **Traditional / Old Model (Pre-AI or Pure Hourly)**
   - Pure Time & Materials (hourly rates × estimated hours)
   - Cost based mainly on human effort
   - Client bears most of the estimation risk
   - Slower delivery, higher cost for the same scope
   - Typical for classic PHP/MySQL/React projects before AI coding agents

   **New AI-Era Models we now offer**
   Present these clearly (use cards, a comparison table, or side-by-side layout):

   a. **AI-Accelerated Fixed Price**
      - Scope is defined → we apply AI velocity (typically 40-70% reduction in hours) → quote a fixed price with contingency
      - Client knows the cost upfront
      - We keep the efficiency gain while still delivering faster

   b. **Value-Based / Outcome-Based Pricing**
      - Price tied to business result (time saved, revenue impact, cost reduction, compliance risk reduced)
      - Often 10-25% of first-year client value or a share of measurable savings

   c. **Hybrid Model (Recommended for most projects)**
      - Fixed price or capped T&M for the core delivery
      - Transparent tracking of AI-assisted vs human-review hours
      - Optional shared-savings clause

   d. **Productized Packages + Monthly Retainer**
      - Clear packages (e.g. “AI-Ready Website + RAG Chat”, “Custom Knowledge Agent”, “IoT + AI Dashboard”)
      - Setup fee + monthly management / optimization / AI monitoring fee

   Use a responsive comparison table or dual-column cards that highlight:
   - What the client pays for
   - Who carries estimation risk
   - Speed of delivery
   - Predictability of cost
   - Typical best-fit scenarios

5. **Example Pricing Ranges** (use placeholder but realistic USD ranges for US/Canada clients)
   - Small AI-accelerated website / internal tool
   - Mid-size custom platform or RAG system
   - Larger multi-agent or IoT + AI solution
   - Monthly retainers for ongoing work
   Clearly mark them as “starting from / typical range — final quote after discovery”

6. **How We Work / Engagement Process**
   - Discovery → Proposal (with chosen pricing model) → Build with AI + human gates → Delivery & knowledge transfer → Optional ongoing support

7. **Final CTA Banner**
   - Strong invitation for US, Canada, UK, Europe & the Middle East clients
   - Primary button: “Book a Discovery Call” or “Get a Tailored Quote”
   - Secondary: “Download Pricing Overview” (optional)

- Be transparent about the AI advantage without overclaiming
- Stress that senior architecture, security review, testing, and client communication remain human-led
- Position the new models as better for the client (faster, more predictable, aligned with value) while protecting healthy margins for the company

Make the Old vs New comparison the clearest and most useful part of the page.
